## 3. Exact contents of private companion archive

Verified actual ZIP, not just repo documentation: **nine required upstream tar archives, subject to new same-commit verification**; **at least 81 third-party notice/license entries, including original Mesa and LLVM texts**; separate original Pillow, Python, PyInstaller and PyMuPDF wheel license evidence; complete OpenSSL notice files; Qt LGPL replacement instructions; build-manifest/native byte-origin and source-mapping reports. The source mapping itself honestly reports `source_archive_hashes_match_exact_native_binary_provenance=false`: matching an upstream tar digest **does not prove** that all original compiler options, patches, bundled Mesa/LLVM or other transitive components are derivable from it.

## 4. Precise unresolved decisions (not bureaucratic requirements)

1. **Exact wheel/transitive licence/source coverage:** resolve whether the provided Mesa/LLVM and image-codec notices completely cover all included code and whether additional *corresponding source* (e.g. Pillow or embedded Mesa/LLVM build inputs) must be supplied under the combined AGPL interpretation. If in doubt, add the needed source/attributions, preserving verified provenance, or get authoritative rights guidance. Do not claim a GPL/LGPL waiver on the strength of ordinary byte checks.
2. **Qt user rights:** confirm recipient's practical access to source and ability to build/run with a modified ABI-compatible Qt on the final release, without special secret keys. No change to `pdf_engine.py` or `app.pyw` is required to investigate.
3. **Exact public source and final checksums:** only after owner-approved `main` merge, produce and review the new same-commit final EXE and source/license bundle; publish the exact source tag and matching source/notice archives concurrently with binary. README download links only after release exists.
4. **Responsible license decision:** a maintainer or competent rights reviewer must explicitly attest that the above applicable rights are met for the **actual final public binary**. Automated CI and this AI review cannot grant copyrights, sign legal terms, or replace the owner’s affirmative decision.
